The problem

Busy small businesses lose time and money at the till

Cash slows everything

Counting money and finding change at every sale builds queues. Cash leaves no record and is a target for theft.

Mobile money disputes

Fake confirmation SMSs, screenshots, reversals and "I've sent it" arguments. Checking each payment holds up the queue.

Records don't add up

Sales sit in a notebook, the till and a phone inbox. At the end of the day owners can't reconcile what they earned.

Owners need an affordable way to accept, verify and record payments without slowing down.

Our solution

One tap: pay, verify and record in one flow

1

Tap

The customer taps a card, key tag or wristband on the reader at the counter or matatu door.

➜
2

M-Pesa prompt

The price is sent as a Daraja STK push to the customer's phone, paid to the business till.

➜
3

Verify

The customer enters their PIN. Hydra Pay marks it PAID only when Safaricom confirms.

➜
4

Record

Saved with amount, time and M-Pesa code. WhatsApp receipt sent, dashboard updated live.

Pay-per-tap: every tap sends one M-Pesa prompt for the price.

Prepaid wallet: regulars top up once, each tap deducts instantly.

Engineering decisions

Reliable reads: firmware debounce, wake-up of half-read cards, 3× retry and max antenna gain (48 dB).
Verified money: PAID only on Safaricom's answer (callback, with status query as backup), recorded exactly once under a lock.
Auditable: append-only ledger; undo is a reversal entry, nothing is deleted. CSV export.
Secure admin: PIN for cards, cash and undo; remote login blocked while the PIN is default.
Self-healing: finds the USB port and reconnects if the reader is unplugged.

Feasibility, security & risks

Risks we've designed for

ChallengeHow Hydra Pay handles it
Fake confirmationsStatus comes only from Safaricom's Daraja API. An unverified payment is never shown as PAID
Reversals & disputesEvery payment keeps its M-Pesa code, status and time in an append-only audit trail; disputes follow Safaricom's process
Slow checkoutTap, then PIN. No typing. Checkout time will be measured in the pilot
Poor networkPending is kept separate from paid, and "still processing" is never marked failed. Offline tap queue is next
Tight budgetsOff-the-shelf ESP32 + RC522, no POS terminal, no per-sale fee
Sensitive dataAdmin PIN, keys kept out of code, minimal data stored; aligned with Kenya's Data Protection Act
